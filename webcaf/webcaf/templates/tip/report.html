Overview

Your Targeted Improvement Plan (TIP) sets out the actions your organisation has committed to take to strengthen its cyber resilience in response to its GovAssure stage 4 review.

It focuses on areas that need improvement to meet the target Government Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) profile.

It lists your priority recommendations and other recommendations, with information you've added for each recommendation, including:

  • description of actions you've committed to
  • action owners
  • whether resources are available
  • whether budget has been approved
  • estimated target completion date
