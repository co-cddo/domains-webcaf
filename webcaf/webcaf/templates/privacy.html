WebCAF privacy notice
Last updated on 26 September 2026.

WebCAF is an online service used by organisations going through GovAssure. To make the service secure and available, we need to collect, process and store some personal data.

  • Our legal basis for processing your data
  • Who we share your data with
  • Where your data is processed and stored
  • How long we store your data
  • How we protect your data and keep it secure
  • Data subject rights
  • Questions and complaints
  • Changes to this notice

What data we collect

The personal data we collect includes:

  • your name
  • the organisation you work for
  • your email address
  • your IP address
  • your browser and connection details

Our legal basis for processing your data

Legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article (6)(1)(f))

When we process your personal data for security monitoring and reporting purposes, the legal basis we rely on is that the processing is necessary for the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology’s (DSIT) legitimate interests. These interests are to:

  • provide access to the service
  • track the changes that people make to information within the service
  • monitor for potential security issues or incidents

Who we share your data with

Your personal data may be shared with security teams in your organisation in relation to security incidents, for example, if malicious behaviour is detected.

We may also share some data for reporting purposes, for example, which organisations currently use WebCAF. Personal data will only be shared internally within DSIT.

Where your data is processed and stored

All processing is based in the UK on DSIT-managed cloud infrastructure using Amazon Web Services (AWS).

How long we store your data

Your personal data is retained so long as you are an active user of this service and for two years for security purposes. IP addresses, browser and connection details are retained for one year for security purposes

Logs are retained for at least 90 days to support deprecation monitoring and incident review.

How we protect your data and keep it secure

We are committed to doing all that we can to keep your information secure. This is why we store and process only the information required for WebCAF to work, to track changes made in the service, and for security monitoring and reporting purposes.

Log data is accessible only to the team operating the service and is not shared with third parties.

All data is encrypted both at rest and in transit.

Data subject rights

You have the right to:

  • request information about how your personal data is processed, and to request a copy of that personal data
  • request that any incomplete personal data is completed, including by means of a supplementary statement
  • request that your personal data is erased if there is no longer a justification for it to be processed
  • request, in certain circumstances (for example, where accuracy is contested), that the processing of your personal data is restricted
  • object to the processing of your personal data where it is processed for direct marketing purposes
  • object to the processing of your personal data
  • withdraw consent to the processing of your personal data at any time
  • request a copy of any personal data you have provided, and for this to be provided in a structured, commonly-used and machine-readable format

Changes to this notice

We may change this privacy notice. In that case the ‘last updated’ date at the top of this page will also change. Any changes to this privacy notice will apply to you and your data immediately.

If these changes affect how your personal data is processed, DSIT will take reasonable steps to let you know.
